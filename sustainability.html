What is Sustainable Tourism?

Sustainable tourism is responsible travel that considers environmental, social and economic impacts. It helps protect natural resources, preserve cultural heritage and support local communities.

Responsible Travel Practices

🌱 Protect Nature

Avoid littering, reduce plastic usage and protect natural environments.

🏛️ Respect Heritage

Follow monument rules and avoid damaging historical structures.

🤝 Support Local People

Buy local products and support small businesses and artisans.

🚲 Eco-Friendly Transport

Choose walking, cycling and public transportation whenever practical.

💧 Save Resources

Use water and electricity responsibly during your travels.

🌍 Reduce Pollution

Minimize waste and avoid activities that harm the environment.

Benefits of Sustainable Tourism

Environmental Protection

Helps conserve natural resources and biodiversity.

Cultural Preservation

Protects traditions, monuments and historical places.

Economic Development

Creates opportunities for local communities and businesses.
